beef-app is a web app which allows users to specify the parameters of a cow, their preference of cuts, and receive an accurate butchering list outlining the specified cuts, their thickness and estimated weight, and the estimated number of cuts.

The application should interactively show the user all available cuts on the cow (in their England / Ireland coloquail names) using a 2D or 3D rendering as appropriate, and then walk them through selecting their cuts,
including which ones are mutually exclusive, which ones can be kept whole or broken down, etc.

Parameters of a cow:
- Sex (Cow / Bull)
- Age
- Weight
- Breed
- Any other relevant butchering parameters from own research

It is up to the implementer to thoroughly research:
- Available cuts when butchering a whole cow or half cow
- Weights, yields, and so on, by breed, sex, age, live weight, etc. (with focus on the Jersey and Aberdeen Angus breeds to begin with)
- Common choices offered by butchers when butchering on behalf of a farm, including:
    - Non-cut options such as stew meat, burger patties, minced / ground beef, and other options for off-cuts
    - Other non-meat parts of the cow which can be returned from the abatoir
    - Organs, bones, etc. which can be returned
    - Thickness
    - Number of steaks / cuts / minced beef / burgers per package

Other useful settings might include e.g. family size (2, 4, 6, or 8 persons) to determine size requirements for roasts.
It is important that the user can select roasts, steaks, or a combination of both depending on the size of the animal and the customer's needs.

Ultimately, the app should help the customer determine:
- What options are available when butchering a beef
- What to request from their butcher (a cut sheet)
- What the estimated yield from their beef is in terms of weight
- What the estimated yield from their beef is in terms of cuts (a list, with counts)
- The best cooking methods for those cuts (wet or dry cook, low or high temp cook, slow or fast cook)
